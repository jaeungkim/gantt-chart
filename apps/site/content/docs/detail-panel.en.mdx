The detail panel is a pane docked to the right of the chart that shows one task at a time. It is
laid out beside the timeline rather than over it, so the timeline narrows while the panel is open.
This page covers turning the panel on, the built-in body, inline editing, `renderDetail` and the
controlled mode.

## Turning the panel on

The panel is off by default. `showDetail` is its switch, as `showTaskList` is the switch for the
pane in [Task list and hierarchy](task-list). When `showDetail` is omitted, passing `renderDetail`
turns the panel on.

| `showDetail` | `renderDetail` | Panel | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| omitted | omitted | hidden | none |
| omitted | given | shown | your renderer |
| `true` | omitted | shown | built-in |
| `true` | given | shown | your renderer |
| `false` | omitted | hidden | none |
| `false` | given | hidden | none |

An explicit `false` wins over `renderDetail`.

Turning the panel on also turns row selection on, so the open task's row is highlighted. An
explicit `selectable={false}` wins over that. The panel opens and closes as usual and no row is
highlighted. [Events](events) describes selection and the callbacks it fires. Props and defaults:
[GanttProps](ref/props).

## The default body

With the panel on and no `renderDetail`, the built-in body shows `task.name` at the top over a
short field list. The name is a text input unless `readOnly` is set on the task or on the chart.

| Caption | Value | Shown | Input when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start, End | The dates, formatted by the current scale's tooltip formatter. `locale` and `formats` control them as they do the hover tooltip. | always | `allowResize` leaves the task resizable |
| Duration | The span between them, in whole hours under a day and whole days from there. The hover tooltip prints the same value. | always | never (derived) |
| Progress | `task.progress`, clamped to 0 to 100. | when the task has one | `allowProgressChange` leaves the progress handle draggable |
| Depends on | The names of the tasks its `dependencies` point at. An id naming no task prints as the id. | when it has any | never (derived) |

The default body shows no other field of the task, and no prop adds a row. The captions are fixed
English strings, as are the accessible names of the panel and its close button. No prop translates
them. To localize the captions or show other fields, render the body with `renderDetail`, below.

## Editing in place

The built-in body is editable where the task's interaction flags allow the matching gesture. The
panel adds no flag of its own.

The name is a text input unless the task, or failing that the chart, is `readOnly`. No narrower
flag covers the name.

Changing a date is a resize, so Start and End are date inputs when the task can be resized. A
summary row's dates stay text. Progress is a number input when the progress handle would be
draggable.

Duration and Depends on are derived and stay text. [Editing tasks](editing) explains how the flags
resolve between task and chart.

A field commits on blur and on `Enter`. The change arrives through `onTasksChange` like every drag,
with the same array and shape and no separate callback. `Escape` reverts the field and leaves the
panel open. A value that cannot commit reverts with no error shown. That covers a blank name, an
incomplete date, an end that is not after the start, and an emptied progress field.

Editing a date replaces the date part only, so the task keeps its time of day. The result is
clamped to the same `minDate` and `maxDate` a drag obeys. Progress is clamped to 0 to 100. While
the dates are editable, the native date input shows `YYYY-MM-DD` in place of the formatted text.
Where resizing is off, the tooltip formatting applies.

## Opening the panel

A single click on a bar or a task list row opens the panel, and so does `Enter` or `Space` on the
focused row. On an expandable row those keys toggle the row instead. There is no double click mode
and no prop that picks another trigger.

The panel opens on a single click because a double click is the convention of modal task editors,
which open a form that must be finished or cancelled. Bryntum's TaskEdit defaults to
`triggerEvent: 'eventdblclick'` and DHTMLX opens its lightbox on `dblclick`. A docked panel opens
on the click that already selects the row and closes with `Escape`, and frappe-gantt's
`popup_trigger` offers only `'click'` and `'hover'`.

`onTaskClick` fires first for that click, and the panel opens after it. Clicking the row that is
already open changes nothing. To let only your own code open the panel, hold `detailTaskId`
yourself as in the controlled mode below. A click only reports through `onDetailChange`, and your
app decides whether to follow it.

The panel is an `<aside role="complementary" aria-label="Task details">` rather than a dialog. It
has no focus trap and no backdrop, and the chart behind it stays interactive. It is outside the
treegrid, so the chart keeps one tab stop and the panel is the next one.

`Escape` closes it with focus anywhere on the page. The listener is on the document and does not
stop the key, so the host's own `Escape` handlers run as well. Closing returns focus to where it
was when the panel opened, unless focus has moved elsewhere on the page since.

The key map gains no new key. `Enter` on a focused row announces the row as before and opens the
panel as well. Every key is listed in [Keyboard and screen readers](accessibility).

## Controlled and uncontrolled

The open task is one id in either mode.

| Prop | Mode | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `detailTaskId` | controlled | The `string` or `null` given here is what the chart shows. Internal state stops updating. |
| `detailTaskId` omitted | uncontrolled | The chart holds the open task itself. |
| `onDetailChange` | both | Fires on every open and close, with the task or `null`. |

The mode switch is `detailTaskId !== undefined`. Here `null` means controlled and closed, not an
opt-out as it is elsewhere.

> [!WARNING]
> In controlled mode the chart opens nothing on its own. It waits for a new `detailTaskId`.
> Passing `detailTaskId` without handling `onDetailChange` makes every click look ignored.

`onDetailChange` fires in both modes, and only when the open task changes. It never fires on
mount, and it does not report changes to the panel's contents. Moving a bar whose detail is open
does not fire it.

## Opening from the handle

`GanttHandle` includes `openDetail(taskId)` and `closeDetail()`. An unknown id is ignored, as
`scrollToTask` ignores one, and both do nothing while the panel is off. In controlled mode they
follow the same rule as a click. They report through `onDetailChange`, and the new `detailTaskId`
is what opens the panel. Both are listed with the rest of the ref in [GanttHandle](ref/handle).

## Replacing the body

`renderDetail` replaces everything inside the panel. The `<aside>` itself stays the chart's, with
its width, its `role`, its accessible name and the focus handling on close. Only the children
change, so the close button is yours to render. `close` is the function the built-in button calls,
and `Escape` keeps working whether you render a button or not.

The renderer receives a `GanttDetailRenderProps` with the open `task` as the chart transformed it,
`close`, the current `scale` and `update`. `update(patch)` patches the open task through the same
commit path as every gesture, the store followed by `onTasksChange`. A custom form can save
without holding the array itself. `update` checks no interaction flags. The rules above apply to
the built-in body only, and a custom body enforces its own rules. The type is in
[Detail renderer](ref/renderers).

This example renders a custom body and holds the open id in state.

```tsx title="ProjectChart.tsx"
import { useState } from 'react';
import { ReactGanttChart } from '@jaeungkim/gantt-chart';
import type { GanttDetailRenderProps, Task } from '@jaeungkim/gantt-chart';
import '@jaeungkim/gantt-chart/style.css';

const tasks: Task[] = [
  { id: 'p1', name: 'Phase 1', parentId: null, sequence: '1',
    startDate: '2025-03-03', endDate: '2025-03-28' },
  { id: 't1', name: 'Design', parentId: 'p1', sequence: '1.1',
    startDate: '2025-03-03', endDate: '2025-03-12', progress: 100 },
  { id: 't2', name: 'Build', parentId: 'p1', sequence: '1.2',
    startDate: '2025-03-12', endDate: '2025-03-28', progress: 40 },
];

function TaskDetail({ task, close }: GanttDetailRenderProps) {
  return (
    <>
      <div style={{ display: 'flex', justifyContent: 'space-between', gap: 8 }}>
        <h2 style={{ margin: 0, fontSize: 14 }}>{task.name}</h2>
        <button type="button" onClick={close} aria-label="Close task details">
          ✕
        </button>
      </div>
      <p style={{ margin: 0, fontSize: 12 }}>
        {task.startDate.slice(0, 10)} → {task.endDate.slice(0, 10)}
      </p>
      <p style={{ margin: 0, fontSize: 12 }}>{task.progress ?? 0}% done</p>
    </>
  );
}

export function ProjectChart() {
  const [detailTaskId, setDetailTaskId] = useState<string | null>(null);

  return (
    <ReactGanttChart
      tasks={tasks}
      showTaskList
      hierarchy
      renderDetail={(props) => <TaskDetail {...props} />}
      detailTaskId={detailTaskId}
      onDetailChange={(task) => setDetailTaskId(task?.id ?? null)}
      height={420}
    />
  );
}
```

`renderDetail` is the chart's only render prop. No other part of the chart accepts a custom
renderer. A bar's colour and class name are fields on the task, described in [Styling](styling).

## Panel width

The panel's width is the custom property `--gantt-detail-width`, listed with its default among
the tokens in [Theming](theming). The panel is laid out as `flex: 0 0` on that value, so it
neither grows nor shrinks and the timeline takes the whole width change.

## Edge cases

- When the open task leaves `tasks`, the panel closes and `onDetailChange` does not fire.
- When `detailTaskId` names a task that is not in the array, the panel stays closed and no error
  is raised. It opens as soon as an id that resolves arrives.
- `Escape` closes the panel only. The row stays selected and `onTaskSelect` does not fire.
- Clicking empty timeline space clears the selection and closes the panel.
- Collapsing the open task's parent leaves the panel open. Ids resolve against every task the
  chart holds, so a hidden row keeps its panel.
- Clicking another row while the panel is open switches the body to that task in place. Focus is
  restored only when the panel unmounts, so it is not pulled back.
- The panel opens on the click rather than on a selection change. Selection ignores a click on
  the already selected row, so after `Escape` a click on the same row reopens the panel.

## Limitations

- The panel does not resize. There is no drag handle and no width prop. `--gantt-detail-width`
  is the only control, and CSS cannot animate it with a spring.
- Placement is fixed on the right. There is no left, bottom or floating variant, and no prop
  detaches the panel into a window of its own.
- The panel shows one task at a time, with no tabs, history, back button or multiple selection.
- Editing stops at the fields the panel shows. Duration and Depends on are never inputs, no prop
  adds a row, and a field the built-in body does not list cannot be edited there. A richer form is
  a `renderDetail` body committing through `update`.
- The slide cannot be configured. The panel opens and closes by transitioning its `flex-basis`
  over 200ms while the timeline reflows, and no prop retimes or disables it.
  `prefers-reduced-motion` makes it instant, like every other transition in the chart. Switching
  tasks while the panel is open swaps the body in place with no slide.

Your app provides any control that toggles `showDetail` and holds the open id in state for
controlled mode. It also implements everything a `renderDetail` form does and persists the panel
width if a user can change it.
