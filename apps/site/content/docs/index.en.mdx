Build an editable project schedule inside your React app. `@jaeungkim/gantt-chart` brings task
dragging, dependency links and nested timelines together in one MIT-licensed component.

Choose a guide for the experience you want to build.

- Change dates and progress on the timeline: [Editing tasks](editing).
- Organize phases and move their tasks together: [Task list and hierarchy](task-list).
- Connect tasks with dependency arrows: [Dependencies](dependencies).
- Account for workweeks and holidays: [Working calendar](working-calendar).
- Match your product's appearance: [Theming](theming).
- Support keyboard editing: [Keyboard and screen readers](accessibility).

Your app receives updated task data through `onTasksChange`.
See [Events](events) for integration and [Introduction](introduction) for scope and limitations.

Start at [Quick start](quick-start). Every prop is listed in [GanttProps](ref/props).
